Privacy Policy (Datenschutzerklärung)

Wingnut Circus is a free browser game. It has no ads, no tracking pixels and no third-party analytics, and it never asks for your name, email address or any other personal details. Below is everything it does process, and why.

Who is responsible

Thomas Wicke, address and contact as in the imprint above. For any privacy question or request, write to wingnutcircus@gmail.com.

Hosting (Cloudflare)

The game and its server run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which processes data on my behalf under a data processing agreement. When you open the game, Cloudflare necessarily receives your IP address and technical request data (browser, time, requested page) to deliver it and to protect it against abuse. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (delivering the game securely).

Stored on your device

The game keeps its data in your browser's local storage, not in cookies: your progress and settings, sound settings, a random device ID, and the login token of your guest account. Without this the game cannot work, so no consent is required (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Guest account and cloud save

So your progress survives updates and can move to other devices, the game creates an anonymous guest account and stores your save on the server. The account has no name or email. With it the server stores the save, the session (including the IP address and browser string at sign-in) and, when you create one, a link code valid for 10 minutes. To limit abuse, IP addresses are also counted briefly when signing in or entering a code. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (providing the game), for abuse protection Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

Game statistics

After each flight the game sends the run to the server: the random device ID, the game version, the craft, mods, scores and how the flights went. No IP address is stored with it. I use this only to balance and improve the game. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (improving the game).

Daily puzzle leaderboard and live rooms

If you submit a daily puzzle score, the nickname you typed is shown publicly on that day's leaderboard together with your score. In live rooms, the other players in the room see your nickname and your plane's position. Don't use your real name if you don't want to. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

Playing on itch.io

If you play the game on itch.io, itch.io is responsible for its own website; see the itch.io privacy policy. The game itself works there as described above.

How long data is kept

Saves and guest accounts are kept while you use them; sessions expire after one year. Game statistics and leaderboard entries are kept while the game is developed. Abuse-protection counters are only needed for minutes. You can ask for deletion at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Email me and, if you can, include your leaderboard nickname or a link code (Profile → ⚙ → Use on another device) so I can find your data. You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one responsible for me: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW), Düsseldorf.
